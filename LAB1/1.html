<h1> is the most important heading and usually represents the main title of a webpage.

About HTML Headings

<h2> is used for major sections under the main heading.

Why Headings Are Important

<h3> helps organize content into meaningful sub-sections.

Improving Readability

<h4> is used for smaller topics within a section.
Structured Content
<h5> makes content more structured and easy to scan.
Minor Notes
<h6> is the smallest heading, used for less important information.

This paragraph is written using the <p> tag, which is used to display normal text on a webpage. Paragraphs help present information clearly and improve readability.

Sometimes, we need to highlight important words. The strong tag (<strong>) is used when the text has importance or meaning.

The bold tag (<b>) simply makes text bold for visual emphasis but does not add importance.

To show stress or emphasis while reading, we use the emphasis tag (<em>).

The italic tag (<i>) is mainly used to display text in italics, such as names, terms, or thoughts.
